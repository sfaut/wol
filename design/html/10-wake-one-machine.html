Where it appears

InfoBars stack bottom-up inside the content area, 16 epx from its bottom edge, 8 epx apart, newest at the bottom, at most 3 (older ones fold into “+2 more”). The row is always updated as well, so nothing depends on the notification alone.

Timing

Status is polled every 2 s while waking. Elapsed time counts up (m:ss). Timeout comes from Settings › Monitoring (default 2:00). Cancel stops waiting only — the packet is already sent — and returns the row to its last checked state.
